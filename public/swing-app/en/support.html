Support Center

Swing Workshop help, troubleshooting and contact information.

Getting started

  1. Record your golf swing face-on or down the line, keeping the camera steady and your whole body visible.
  2. Import the video from the Swing tab and run the analysis.
  3. Review key swing phases, possible faults and suggested Motor Skills, Body Prep and Club Fitting guidance.
  4. Open History to compare previous analysis results.

Frequently asked questions

My analysis looks inaccurate. Results depend on camera position, lighting, occlusion and video quality. Try recording again with a stable camera and your full body in frame.

How do I change photo access? Manage the app's permissions in iPhone Settings.

Questions about PRO? Check the in-app purchase and restore options where available, then contact support if the issue persists.

Contact support

Email clubmakers@naver.com with your device model, iOS version, app version, steps to reproduce and a description of the issue. Please do not send sensitive recordings unless requested.
